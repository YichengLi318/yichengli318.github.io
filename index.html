    A Closed-loop subsidy allocation

    03 Research & Personal Projects open source

    Activation-FFT

    What does a language model look like in the frequency domain?

    Interpretability work on language models has mostly examined one activation at a time. Activation-FFT takes the Fourier transform of the hidden states along the token axis, so a whole layer can be read as a spectrum rather than as a set of isolated activations.

    • An invertible edit hook applies an arbitrary spectral transform at any layer and inverts it back into activations, so an edit in the frequency domain becomes a real forward pass.
    • Across several benchmarks, edits to different frequency bands drive distinct and reproducible shifts in the output — evidence that the bands carry functionally separable information rather than one smear of signal.
    • The transform runs along the token axis per layer and per hidden dimension, so the spectrum is a property of the sequence at that depth, not of the weights.

    The six pillars standing in front of the terrain are the band energies, and they are the control. Close one and the trajectories behind them are rebuilt from the edited spectrum.

    Physics-Specialized LLM

    An 8B model that answers physics like a 70B one

    I built a physics-specialized corpus, fine-tuned a small LLM, and put a retrieval layer underneath.

    • Built a physics knowledge dataset, PHOTON (Physics Hybrid Open Training Of Networks, available on Kaggle), and fine-tuned a Llama-3.1-8B model on it using LoRA with Unsloth.
    • Organised a retrieval-augmented generation framework, embedded the knowledge with SBERT for vectorisation, and built a vector database with FAISS, enabling efficient retrieval of relevant information.
    • Tested on multiple tasks including the GPQA benchmark, reaching an overall accuracy of 68.2% with the 8B model, comparable to a 70B model.

    Hamiltonians Neural Network for Degenerate Crystals

    Undergraduate Research Assistant, Tsinghua University

    Beijing, ChinaMar – Jun 2023

    • Proved that the existing networks produce the wrong Hamiltonian — the property the whole method exists to predict — for any crystal with degenerate energy bands. When two bands coincide the eigenvectors stop being well defined, and the gradient the network trains on is not the gradient of anything.
    • Proposed an auto-differentiation algorithm built on linear algebra that stays correct through the degeneracy, accommodating 80+ elements of the periodic table, and reimplemented it on SciPy and JAX.
    • Validated on classical crystal configurations including GaN and GeS, where it beat the previous method on both versatility and tolerance to hardware precision.
